## Tuning, round 2: fixes for the slope collapse

The full matrix of round 1 found `lin_s10000_l4_t0.05_d` diverging on CPU-sharing backends near saturation. In overload, a request sent while a server has few connections sits through the flood that follows, so response time falls with the connections seen at dispatch; the fitted slope clamps to 0, the estimate stops depending on connections, and requests herd onto one server at a time while the others sit idle with stale estimates. Two fixes: a slope floor `b >= floor * a` (`_f<floor>`), and fitting on the average number of connections over the request's life (`_avg`). Same 9 scenarios, now also at 95 % load (5 seeds × 100,000). Chosen: the best mean among variants whose worst cell is at most +15 %: `lin_s10000_l1_t0.05_d_f0.25_avg`.
