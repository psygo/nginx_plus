# Exploration #4: admission control and queueing at nginx

See the docstring of `sim/explore/admission.py`. 10 paired seeds × 200,000 requests, patched least_time (`bucket=5ms smooth=3s`) in every case. `reject:K` is `max_conns=K` in open-source nginx; `queue:K:T` is NGINX Plus `queue` with `max_conns=K` and a timeout of T ms. For slowdown, p99 is over the 20 s slow window. Load 1.05 is overload: more requests than the servers can handle.
